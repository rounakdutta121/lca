What is JSS 55555?

JSS 55555 is a leading environmental standard for testing the resistance of electronic and electrical equipment against mold growth and extreme environmental conditions.

It is widely used in military, defense, and aerospace electronics to ensure survival in the harshest environments on Earth.

JSS 55555 vs MIL-STD 810

Feature JSS 55555 (Penta-Five) MIL-STD 810
Primary Focus Mold resistance, environmental stress Environmental & mechanical stress
Target Products Electronics & electrical equipment Military, aerospace, and electronics
Global Acceptance India & select international markets Worldwide, highly recognized
Main Objective Required for Indian defense market Accepted in all global military contracts

* Takeaway: JSS 55555 is essential for electronics targeting Indian defense, while aligning closely with international MIL-STD 810.
